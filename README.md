# Stein Combat

Clans, allies and the nearest enemy, for the **[Stein Loader](https://github.com/x4vieer/stein-labs)** (Minecraft
1.8.9 on Java 25).

**[Download Combat.steinmod](https://github.com/x4vieer/stein-combat/releases/latest/download/Combat.steinmod)** —
drop it into the `mods` folder of your `.minecraft` and restart the game. Turn it on and off in the Stein Loader
menu; everything about it is configured on the Combat page of the Panel (Insert).

- **Allies you cannot hit by accident**: players and clans on a list, or the Y key aiming at someone (Shift+Y for
  their clan). A hit on an ally is cancelled before it reaches the server.
- **Hide allies (H)**: your clan and your allies stop being drawn — body, armor, nametag and shadow — so you can
  see who is hitting you in a pile-up. Press it again and everyone comes back.
- **Colors by relation**: your clan, allies, enemies and everyone else get their own color in the Tab list, above
  the head and on the minimap. Enemy needs proof: only who you marked, or who the server's clan plugin puts in a
  clan other than yours.
- **Nearest enemy** on the HUD grid, and a sprint toggle key.

Source is not public. Releases here are published by CI.
